# 📊 YouTube Trending Video Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

YouTube is one of the world's largest video-sharing platforms, where millions of videos compete for audience attention every day. Understanding what makes a video trend can provide valuable insights for content creators, digital marketers, and businesses.

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on YouTube Trending Video Statistics using Python. The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in audience engagement, content popularity, and relationships between key metrics such as views, likes, comments, subscribers, and video categories.

The project demonstrates the complete EDA workflow, including data understanding, preprocessing, visualization, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze the characteristics of trending YouTube videos.
- Understand audience engagement through views, likes, and comments.
- Identify the most frequently trending channels and video categories.
- Explore relationships among important variables.
- Detect missing values and outliers.
- Generate meaningful business insights using Exploratory Data Analysis.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Most trending videos receive moderate view counts, while only a small percentage become viral.
- Videos with higher views generally receive more likes and comments.
- Audience engagement metrics show strong positive relationships.
- Popular channels frequently appear in the trending section.
- Subscriber count contributes to visibility but does not guarantee high video performance.
- Most trending videos allow comments, encouraging audience interaction.
